## 🎯 Detailed Syllabus Map (Last 2.5 Months)

### 1. Quarter 2 Theme: Recreation and Entertainment

#### A. Supermarket & Mall
* **Key Concepts**: What is a supermarket? Different sections (groceries, fruits/vegetables, snacks, household items). How shopping lists work.
* **Workbook Activities**:
  * **Page 22**: Supermarket items crossword puzzle using picture clues.
  * **Page 23**: Connecting shopping list items in sequence to navigate out of the supermarket maze.

#### B. Amusement Park vs. Theme Park
* **Amusement Park**: A place with various rides and games designed for fun.
  * *Rides Learned*: Ferris Wheel 🎡, Carousel 🎠, Roller Coaster 🎢, Bumper Cars 🚗, Merry-go-round.
* **Theme Park**: A park where all rides, architecture, and attractions are built around a specific central theme or story (e.g., Disney/Superhero theme).

#### C. Movie Theatre (Cinema Hall)
* **Key Concepts**: Key features of a movie theatre—Big Screen 📽️, Comfortable Seats 🪑, Ticket Counter 🎟️, Movie Tickets, Snack Counter & Popcorn 🍿.
* **Field Trip Experience**: School outing to *Movie Maxx* on September 7, 2026, to watch **Spiderman**.
* **Art Activity**: Drawing and colouring "Things I See at a Movie Theatre" on art sheets.

#### D. Community Helpers & Field Trip
* **Educational Field Trip**: Visit to the **Police Station**.
* **Learnings**: Role of police officers in keeping the neighbourhood safe, reporting emergencies, traffic safety, and community awareness.

### 2. Quarter 1 Theme: Myself & Human Body

#### A. Internal & External Body Organs
* **External Organs**: Eyes, ears, nose, hands, legs.
* **Internal Organs**:
  * **Brain**: Thinks and controls the body.
  * **Heart**: Pumps blood.
  * **Lungs**: Help us breathe air.
  * **Stomach**: Digests food.
  * **Bones & Muscles**: Support and move our body.

#### B. Daily Routine & Healthy Habits
* **Daily Routine Steps**: Waking up, brushing teeth, bathing, eating healthy breakfast, going to school, playing, studying, sleeping early.
* **FDA/FSSAI Healthy Food Guidelines**:
  * *Healthy Tiffin*: Fresh fruits, vegetables, idli, dosa, poha, upma, chana, paneer, curd, buttermilk, oats/millet.
  * *Avoid HFSS (High Fat, Sugar, Salt)*: Chips, instant noodles, chocolates, deep-fried snacks, soft drinks, packaged sugary juices.

## ✏️ Interactive Home Discussion & Quiz Questions

Ask these verbal practice questions to test your child's understanding:

### Quiz Section 1: Places of Recreation
1. *"What is the difference between an Amusement Park and a Theme Park?"*
2. *"Name 4 rides you can ride at an amusement park!"* (Ferris Wheel, Carousel, Bumper Cars, Roller Coaster)
3. *"What are 4 things you see inside a Movie Theatre?"* (Big screen, tickets, seats, popcorn)
4. *"Where do we buy groceries and vegetables together under one big roof?"* (Supermarket)

### Quiz Section 2: Body Organs
1. *"Which internal organ pumps blood in our body?"* (Heart)
2. *"Which organ helps us think?"* (Brain)
3. *"What do our lungs do?"* (Help us breathe)
4. *"Where does the food go after we swallow it?"* (Stomach)

### Quiz Section 3: Healthy Living
1. *"Name 3 healthy foods you should put in your school tiffin!"* (Fruits, Poha/Idli, Paneer/Nuts)
2. *"Why should we avoid eating too many chocolates and chips?"* (Bad for health, teeth, and energy)
