## 🎯 Detailed Syllabus Map (Last 2.5 Months)

### 1. Visual Arts (Art & Craft)

#### A. Colour Mixing & Secondary Colours
* **Primary Colours**: Red, Blue, Yellow.
* **Secondary Colour Experiments**:
  * `Red` + `Yellow` = **Orange**
  * `Blue` + `Yellow` = **Green**
  * `Red` + `Blue` = **Purple / Violet**
* **Technique**: Tissue paper dabbing with red and yellow poster paints.

#### B. Artistic Style: Kandinsky Concentric Circles
* Inspired by famous artist Wassily Kandinsky.
* **Technique**: Painting concentric circles starting from a small center dot and expanding outward with multi-coloured rings.

#### C. Texture & Pattern Printmaking
* **Bottle Cap Stamping**: Creating textured round prints using bottle caps dipped in paint.
* **Shape Patterns**: Creating repeating geometric patterns using shapes.

### 2. Music (Harmonium & Vocal Sargam)
* **Instrument Introduced**: **Harmonium**.
* **Basic Sargam Practice**: `Sa - Re - Ga - Ma - Pa - Dha - Ni - Sa`
* **Skills Developed**: Finger coordination, pitch recognition, listening skills, and rhythmic tempo.

### 3. Library & Storytelling Sessions

The school library conducted rich storytelling and guided reading sessions:

| Book Title | Author | Core Theme / Learning Outcome |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **The Very Hungry Caterpillar** | Eric Carle | Life cycle of a butterfly, 7 days of the week, healthy food vs junk food. |
| **Night Monkey, Day Monkey** | Julia Donaldson | Day and night difference, nocturnal vs diurnal animals. |
| **Lola at the Library** | Anna McQuinn | How a library works, choosing books, library card & care of books. |
| **Oliver's Vegetables** | Vivian French | Healthy eating habits and exploring different vegetables. |
| **Fast Fox and Slow Snail** | Moral Story | Concept of Opposites (*Fast* vs. *Slow*). |
| **The Milkmaid** | Aesop's Fable | Moral lesson: Don't count your chickens before they hatch. |

### 4. Dance & Celebrations
* **Lezim Dance**: Traditional Maharashtrian folk dance with Lezim props performed during the Ganesh Chaturthi celebration.
* **World Dot Day Activity**: Creative dance & art movement session.

### 5. Horticulture (Botany & Nature Study)
* **Garden Visit**: Guided walk in the school garden inspecting real plants.
* **Leaf Study**: Examining indoor plant leaves—comparing smooth vs rough textures, leaf veins, big leaves vs small leaves.
* **Flower Inspection**: Observing real flowers (Red Rose, White Rose, Pink Rose, Lotus) to learn petal count, colour, and scent.

### 6. Sports & Physical Education
* **Physical Fitness Routines**: Warm-up stretches, cool-down exercises.
* **Games**: Obstacle course races, Bean Bag collection race, agility drills.

### 7. Yoga & Movement Practice
* **Concept**: Yoga promotes body awareness, balance, calmness, focus, and flexibility.
* **Common Activities**: Stretching, breathing exercises, simple poses, and guided relaxation.

### 8. Circle Time & Social Learning
* **Purpose**: Build confidence, listening skills, turn-taking, and communication.
* **Activities**: Sharing stories, discussing feelings, participating in classroom conversations, and daily greetings.

### 9. Children’s Council / Democratic Activities
* **Concept**: Learners participate in simple democratic processes and role-play activities.
* **Observed Practice**: Election discussions, student participation, voting-style activities, and civic awareness.

### 10. Reading Program
* **Focus**: Reading with pointed tracking, expression, recognition of key words, and simple retelling.
* **Purpose**: Builds confidence in sight reading and comprehension through repeated exposure to stories.

## 🎨 Creative Home Activities & Reading Prompts

1. **Colour Mixing at Home**: Give your child water colours. Let them mix Red and Yellow on paper and discover Orange!
2. **Kandinsky Circle Painting**: Draw concentric circles together using oil pastels or watercolours.
3. **Sing the Sargam**: Practice singing `Sa Re Ga Ma Pa Dha Ni Sa` together at home.
4. **Bedtime Story Retelling**: Pick any storybook at home and ask your child: *"Can you point with your pointer finger and retell me what happens in this story?"*
5. **Yoga at Home**: Practice a few simple stretching poses and breathing exercises.
6. **Circle Time Practice**: Ask your child to say one happy thing, one sad thing, and one interesting fact from the day.
