Parents should use this map as a print-friendly reference and revise the child in short, consistent daily sessions. Focus on:

- listening and speaking
- reading full words clearly
- writing neatly from dictation
- counting and recognizing number patterns
- checking concept understanding through oral questions
- discussing and revising classroom experiences at home

This structure keeps revision manageable and ensures regular progress across all subjects.
