## 🎯 Detailed Syllabus Map (Last 2.5 Months)

### 1. Earth's Systems: Muddy Puddles & Evaporation
* **Core Concept**: Why do muddy puddles disappear after rain?
* **Scientific Explanation**:
  * Heat from the **Sun** heats up the water in the puddle.
  * Water turns into invisible **water vapour** (gas) and rises into the air.
  * This process is called **Evaporation**.
* **Video Reference**: [Evaporation for Kids Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SWmLBGSJWyE&t=31s)

### 2. Force & Motion (Bowling Experiment)
* **Core Concept**: **Force** is a **Push** or a **Pull** that causes an object to move, change speed, or change direction.
* **Hands-On Demonstration**: Bowling activity where rolling a ball (applying a push force) knocks over bowling pins.
* **Observation**: The more force applied, the greater the movement.

### 3. Gravity
* **Core Concept**: **Gravity** is the invisible pulling force that holds us to the ground and causes dropped objects to fall toward Earth instead of floating away.
* **Everyday Examples**: Apple falling from a tree, ball dropping to the ground.

### 4. Astronomy & Constellations (Cosmos Dome Planetarium)
* **Cosmos Dome Event (August 20, 2026)**:
  * Full dome planetarium experience exploring the Big Bang theory, origin of planets, stars, and galaxies.
* **Starry Constellations**:
  * Understanding that **Constellations** are groups of stars forming recognizable patterns or shapes in the night sky (e.g., Big Dipper).
  * Connect-the-dots star pattern activity.

### 5. Density Tower Experiment
* **Core Concept**: Liquids have different **densities** (weights). Heavy liquids sink to the bottom; light liquids float on top.
* **Experiment Layers**:
  1. **Honey** (Densest / Heaviest ➔ Sinks to the bottom)
  2. **Water** (Medium density ➔ Sits in the middle)
  3. **Oil** (Least dense / Lightest ➔ Floats on top)
* **Observation**: Different liquids do not mix easily because their densities differ.

### 6. Light & Shadows
* **Core Concept**: A **shadow** is formed when an opaque object blocks light from passing through.
* **Hands-on Activity**: Using torches and objects to observe how shadow size changes when the light source moves closer or farther.

### 7. Sound: Loud vs. Soft
* **Core Concept**: Differentiating sound volume:
  * **Loud Sounds**: Car horn, thunder, drum roll.
  * **Soft Sounds**: Whispering, rustling leaves, clock ticking.

### 8. Skittles Rainbow Experiment
* **Core Concept**: Dissolving food dye and sugar in water. Placing Skittles in a circle on a plate and pouring warm water creates a colorful rainbow swirl toward the center.

## 🔬 Easy At-Home Experiments to Practice with Your Child

```
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| EXPERIMENT 1: The Disappearing Puddle                                   |
| Put a small bowl of water outside in the sun. Check it after 3 hours.  |
| Ask: "Where did the water go?" -> EVAPORATION!                          |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| EXPERIMENT 2: Liquid Density Glass                                      |
| Pour honey, water with food color, and cooking oil in a clear glass.   |
| Observe the 3 distinct layers!                                         |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| EXPERIMENT 3: Torch Shadow Puppet                                       |
| Darken the room, turn on flashlight, and make hand shadow shapes on wall|
| Ask: "Why is a shadow made?" -> The hand BLOCKS the light!             |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```
