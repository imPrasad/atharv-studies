## 🎯 Detailed Syllabus Map (Last 2.5 Months)

### 1. Short Vowels (CVC Words)
* **Vowels Covered**: Short `a`, `e`, `i`, `o`, `u`
* **Word Families Practised**:
  * **Short `a`**: `cat`, `hat`, `bag`, `cap`, `fan`
  * **Short `e`**: `bed`, `pen`, `ten`, `net`, `hen`
  * **Short `i`**: `bib`, `lid`, `pig`, `fin`, `lip`, `sit`, `dim`, `mix`
  * **Short `o`**: `job`, `sob`, `pod`, `dog`, `log`, `hop`, `pot`, `fox`
  * **Short `u`**: `cub`, `rub`, `mud`, `bug`, `hut`, `sun`, `cup`, `gum`

### 2. Rhyming Words
* **Concept**: Rhyming words have the same ending sound.
* **Examples**:
  * `cat`, `hat`, `mat`
  * `pig`, `big`, `dig`
  * `hop`, `top`, `shop`
* **Class Activity**: Listening to ending sounds and matching rhyming pairs.

### 3. Consonant Blends (L-Blends)
* **`pl` Blends**: `plum`, `plan`, `plot`, `plug`
* **`sl` Blends**: `slow`, `slug`, `slim`, `slab`
* **`gl` Blends**: `glad`, `glow`, `glue`, `globe`
* **`cl` Blends**: `clap`, `clay`, `clip`, `club`
* **`bl` Blends**: `blur`, `blue`, `blow`, `block`
* **`fl` Blends**: `flow`, `flop`, `flap`, `flip`

### 4. Long Vowels & Magic "e" Concept
Learners were introduced to how a silent **Magic 'e'** changes a short vowel sound into a long vowel sound where the vowel says its name:
* **Short vs Long Transformation**:
  * `pin` ➔ `pine`
  * `kit` ➔ `kite`
  * `fin` ➔ `fine`
  * `hop` ➔ `hope`
  * `not` ➔ `note`
  * `rob` ➔ `robe`
  * `cub` ➔ `cube`
  * `tub` ➔ `tube`
  * `mut` ➔ `mute`
* **Long Vowel 'a'**: `cake`, `name`, `game`, `lane`, `date`
* **Long Vowel 'e'**: `theme`, `these`, `green`, `tree`, `bee`

### 5. Digraphs
* **`sh` Digraph**: `ship`, `shop`, `shed`, `fish`, `dish`
* **`ch` Digraph**: `chin`, `chop`, `chat`, `chip`, `chest`
* **`oo` Digraphs**:
  * **Short `oo` sound**: `book`, `look`, `took`, `foot`
  * **Long `oo` sound**: `moon`, `room`, `zoo`, `soon`

### 6. Reading Program & Fitzroy Readers
The child has read and practiced the following readers with proper expression, pronunciation, and pointer finger tracking:
1. **"The Animals"** (Fitzroy Reader)
2. **"The Frog and the Fly"** (Fitzroy Reader)
3. **"The Lost Bear"** (Fitzroy Reader)
4. **Reading practice**: pointing to words while reading, following text from left to right, and retelling the story in simple language.

## ✏️ Home Practice Exercises & Mock Drills

### Practice 1: Dictation Mock Tests (Daily 10 Minutes)
Dictate 5 random words from the target list and check spelling & handwriting:
* **Set A (Long 'i')**: `pine`, `bike`, `kite`, `pipe`, `hive`
* **Set B (Long 'o')**: `robe`, `hope`, `note`, `dome`, `rose`
* **Set C (Long 'u')**: `cube`, `mule`, `tube`, `cute`, `tune`
* **Set D (Long 'a' and 'e')**: `cake`, `game`, `name`, `tree`, `bee`

### Practice 2: Rhyming Words Drill
Say a word and ask your child to find a rhyming partner:
1. `cat` → ____________
2. `big` → ____________
3. `hop` → ____________
4. `bee` → ____________
5. `pen` → ____________

### Practice 3: Magic 'e' Transformation Sheet
Ask your child to add 'e' at the end of each word and read the new word aloud:
1. `pin` + `e` = ____________
2. `hop` + `e` = ____________
3. `cub` + `e` = ____________
4. `kit` + `e` = ____________
5. `not` + `e` = ____________
6. `tub` + `e` = ____________
7. `mat` + `e` = ____________
8. `cap` + `e` = ____________

### Practice 4: Blend & Digraph Identification
Circle the correct blend/digraph for each word:
1. 🍇 `[ pl / sl ]` `um` (plum)
2. 🐌 `[ sl / gl ]` `ug` (slug)
3. 🧱 `[ bl / cl ]` `ock` (block)
4. ⛵ `[ sh / ch ]` `ip` (ship)
5. 📚 `[ oo / ch ]` `k` (book)
